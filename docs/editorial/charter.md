# NomosX Editorial Charter

*Internal charter — injected into writer and judge prompts. Source: consolidated
chantier §4 (post run-12). Version 1.0.*

## Promise

Every note answers **one dated question with a probability** — with its engines,
its indicators, and what it means for a decision-maker. European vantage point.
A note that cannot name its question, its horizon, and its probability does not
publish.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast note | 1 500 – 2 500 words | 24 – 72 h after the signal |
| Prospective note | 3 000 – 3 500 words | Deep-dive; 2 – 3 scenarios whose probabilities sum to 100 % |

**Hard cap: 3 500 words** in the published version. Detail lives in collapsible
annexes, never in the main text.

## Domains (radar verticals)

1. Geopolitics & security
2. International economics & finance
3. Energy & climate
4. Tech & AI
5. Europe & politics

## Language & cadence

- Publication language: **English**; automatic French version.
- **3 – 5 notes per week maximum. No quota.**
- **3 subjects per cycle** — spend concentrates on the best verified questions.

## Note anatomy (mandatory blocks)

1. **Title / chapeau = the forecast question itself** — never an invented headline.
2. **Dateline**: `P = x % by [horizon]` from the forecaster ensemble (never an
   analyst guess), market odds when available, and **"Information as of
   [date]"** (latest source date or run date, OFCE-style).
3. Bottom line + key judgments.
4. Evidence and causal chain.
5. Scenarios with 90 % intervals; central scenario block.
6. **Key assumptions** — dedicated block ("if X, then…"), PIIE-style.
7. **Actors: options → likely reactions** — the verified actor map rendered,
   Montaigne-style.
8. Trajectory + signals to watch.
9. **"What We Ruled Out"** — includes a *false leads* line naming recurring
   media narratives judged improbable (Eurasia Group-style).
10. Decision section + sources + method colophon.

## Confidence

Confidence is **computed**, not self-declared: coverage, full-text share, and
forecaster-family agreement → `low / medium / high` (Stimson-style). The
self-declared `position.confidence` score never ships in the dateline. The
method box must never contradict the computed figures.

## Hard rules

- Publication happens **only on signals**; no human re-read.
- A published note is **never modified** — resolutions live in the registry.
- Zero unsourced statistical numbers in the published artifact.
- At least one real fetched series per forecast note.
- Forecast contracts (§1.6) are hard-fail motifs at the barrier.
